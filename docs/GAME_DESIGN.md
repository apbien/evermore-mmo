# Purpose

This document defines the foundational philosophy of Evermore.

It establishes principles for:

- worldbuilding
- progression
- discovery
- skills
- disciplines
- player identity
- long-term MMO design

Individual systems should expand upon this document.

---

# High Concept

Evermore is a persistent MMORPG centered around discovery, mastery, and personal identity.

Players grow by understanding the world.

Progression is not defined only by:

- levels
- equipment
- numerical power
- optimization

A player's identity is shaped by:

- what they discover
- what they learn
- how they specialize
- how they express their abilities

---

# Core Philosophy

## Understanding Is Progression

Information alone is not mastery.

Knowing something exists is different from understanding how to use it.

Evermore rewards:

- experimentation
- observation
- application
- specialization

---

## The World Should Be Discoverable, Not Solvable

Evermore accepts that players will:

- create wikis
- share discoveries
- optimize systems
- publish guides

The goal is not preventing information sharing.

The goal is ensuring knowledge does not remove depth.

Knowledge should expand possibilities rather than reduce the game into a checklist.

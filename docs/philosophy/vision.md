# Vision

This document defines the foundational philosophy of Evermore. Individual
systems expand from here. This is the "why" behind everything else.

## High Concept

Evermore is a persistent MMORPG centered around discovery, mastery,
specialization, personal identity, and player expression.

Players grow by understanding the world. Progression is not defined only by
level, equipment, damage output, optimization, or completion.

A player's identity is shaped by what they discover, what they learn, how they
specialize, how they express their abilities, and what role they choose in the
world.

> The game can be documented, but it cannot be exhausted.

Players may discover what exists. They should never stop wondering what is
possible.

## Understanding Is Progression

Evermore rewards understanding rather than simple accumulation. Knowing that
something exists is different from mastering it.

A player may read about a hidden ability, a rare creature, a crafting technique,
or an unexplored location. Knowledge alone does not create mastery. True
progression comes from experimentation, practice, application, adaptation, and
specialization.

A guide can reveal possibilities. A guide cannot replace experience.

## Character Growth Philosophy

Evermore characters should grow horizontally as much as vertically. A player
should not only become stronger — they should become more understood.

Progression should create more choices, more expression, more specialization,
and more opportunities. A character's history should matter.

## Core Principle

Evermore is a world where knowledge spreads, discoveries become history, and
players are remembered for how they changed their understanding of Arkadion.

The game can be documented. The world cannot be exhausted.

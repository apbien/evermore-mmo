# Professions

Professions are production-based classes, separate from Disciplines. A player's
combat identity and production identity do not need to match — a Vanguard can be a
master smith, an Arcanist a carpenter, a Ranger a legendary chef.

## Profession Philosophy

Professions represent a player's contribution to civilization. They answer: *What
do I create?*

## Examples

**Blacksmith** — weapons, armor, tools, specialized materials.

**Alchemist** — potions, compounds, magical preparations. (Note: Art catalyst
items — see `unlocks-and-discovery.md` — are a natural fit for a
crafting Profession, tying production directly into the Art economy.)

**Carpenter** — structures, furniture, ships, tools.

**Tailor** — clothing, specialized equipment, cosmetic items.

## Profession Progression

Professions progress through knowledge, recipes, experimentation, rare materials,
discoveries, and reputation.

A master crafter should be known because of what they have created — not because
everyone eventually reaches the same crafting level.

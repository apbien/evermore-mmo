# Disciplines

## Purpose

Disciplines replace traditional MMO classes. A Discipline defines a player's
primary combat identity and determines combat philosophy, available Arts,
Discipline progression, unique mechanics, role specialization, and preferred
playstyles.

A Discipline is not simply a collection of abilities. It represents the approach a
character has developed toward solving problems in the world.

Discipline also serves a second, mechanical role: **it is the access key for
Arts.** A Discipline owns a list of Arts, and a player can only unlock and equip
Arts belonging to their currently-active Discipline. See `arts.md`.

## Discipline Philosophy

Traditional MMO classes often define the entire character. Evermore separates
identity into layers. A Vanguard is always a Vanguard — but no two Vanguards must
be identical. One may become an immovable defender, another a battlefield
commander, another an aggressive frontline warrior, another a protector focused
on allies.

The Discipline establishes the foundation. The player creates the expression.

## Discipline-Native Skills

Each Discipline has a set of native skills — a guaranteed identity **floor**.
These are learned by leveling the Discipline, not through open discovery, so a
Discipline always functions even if the player has discovered nothing. Native
skills are the exception to the discovery-based skill model in
`skills.md`.

## Switching Disciplines

Switching Disciplines is **free** — there is no respec cost. Switching changes
which Arts are currently equippable, but never destroys unlock progress. Unlocked
Arts and mastered Skills are permanent (see `arts.md` and
`unlocks-and-discovery.md`).

Progress toward unlocking a Discipline's Arts can only be made while that
Discipline is active. You may grind an unlock, then leave and keep it — but you
cannot progress every Discipline's Arts simultaneously.

## Example Discipline: Vanguard

**Concept:** a warrior dedicated to controlling danger and protecting others.

Possible characteristics: defensive combat, battlefield control, endurance,
threat management, protection.

Possible Arts: Bulwark, Guardian, Bastion, Warlord.

A Vanguard may fulfill a traditional tank role, but the player determines the
exact style through Arts, Skills, and equipment.

## Roles Without Forced Classes

Evermore allows players to fulfill traditional MMO roles without being
permanently locked into them. A player wanting to tank may choose a Vanguard with
defensive Arts and protection Skills; another may tank using a magical Discipline
with support Arts and defensive Skills. The role exists; the identity remains
flexible.

# First Discoveries

Being first matters. But first discovery should create **prestige, not permanent
ownership.**

## Discovery Recognition

The first player or group to discover something may receive historical records,
titles, achievements, reputation, cosmetic recognition, and permanent
documentation.

## Prestige Over Exclusivity

Discoveries should eventually become available to the wider community. The
original discoverers are remembered because they changed history — not because
they prevent others from participating.

This is the reward structure that makes **consumable secrets** worthwhile even
though they get wikied quickly (see `../systems/unlocks-and-discovery.md`): the
person who finds a one-time secret first earns the permanent record, and the
secret then rightly becomes shared knowledge.

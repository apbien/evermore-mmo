# Knowledge Economy

Evermore accepts that discoveries spread. Players will create guides, wikis,
communities, and databases. This is part of the game, not a failure.

## Information as a Resource

Knowledge has value. Players may discover, preserve, teach, trade, verify, and
expand information.

Information should not simply remove gameplay. A guide should let players
*attempt* something; it does not remove the need for understanding. Knowing that
a Skill exists is not mastering it. Knowing where something is located is not
understanding it. Knowing a recipe exists is not becoming a master crafter.

(This is the social-facing counterpart to the repeatable-secret design in
`../systems/unlocks-and-discovery.md`: shared knowledge is an invitation to do, not
a substitute for doing.)

## Knowledge Roles

Different players may become known for different forms of knowledge:

- **Researchers** — investigate systems and mechanics
- **Historians** — preserve the story of Arkadion
- **Explorers** — discover unknown locations
- **Teachers** — specialize in sharing knowledge with others
- **Archivists** — maintain records of discoveries

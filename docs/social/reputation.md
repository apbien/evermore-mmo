# Reputation

Reputation represents how the world and other players recognize accomplishments.
It should reflect meaningful actions.

## Social Identity

Evermore encourages players to develop identities beyond combat power. A player's
reputation should come from meaningful contributions. A player may become known
as the first explorer of a forgotten continent, a master blacksmith, a famous
researcher, a legendary monster hunter, a respected teacher, or an expert
cartographer.

The goal is many paths to recognition.

## Reputation Sources

Players may gain reputation through exploration, discoveries, crafting
achievements, research, combat accomplishments, helping communities, and
completing major events.

## Reputation Philosophy

Reputation should answer *"What has this player contributed?"* — not *"How much
power does this player have?"*

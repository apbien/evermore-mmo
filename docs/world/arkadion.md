# Arkadion

## World Philosophy

Arkadion is an ancient world that existed before the player. Players are
explorers of an existing world, not its authors.

They uncover forgotten histories, lost civilizations, hidden locations, unusual
creatures, forgotten techniques, and unexplained phenomena. Players do not create
the history of Arkadion — they become part of it.

The world should feel larger than any individual player. There should always be
places that have not been understood.

## Civilization

Major settlements are designated as **Havens**. Each Haven has a numerical
designation and a historical name. The numerical system provides practical
communication; the historical name provides identity and cultural meaning.

Examples:

- Haven I: Hearth
- Haven II: Brookmere
- Haven X: Everlight

Players may refer to settlements using either designation.

## Everlight

Everlight is the Grand Capital of Arkadion. It represents the center of
civilization, the foundation of recorded knowledge, and the connection between
the known world and unexplored regions.

Everlight is not the end of exploration. It is the place where explorers return
to share what they have found.

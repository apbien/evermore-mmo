# Cartography

Cartography is the discipline of documenting and understanding Arkadion.
Cartographers are not simply map makers — they are explorers, historians,
researchers, and archivists.

## Cartography Philosophy

Maps are records of understanding. A map should communicate more than geography.

A complete cartographic record may include terrain information, discovered
locations, historical notes, resource information, creature observations,
expedition records, and environmental changes.

## Cartography Progression

Cartography improves through exploration, observation, documentation, research,
comparison, and verification.

A player does not become a master cartographer by walking everywhere. They become
one by understanding the world.

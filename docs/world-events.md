# World Events

World events are changes that occur within Arkadion. They reinforce that the
world exists independently of individual players.

## Philosophy

Events should not simply be temporary content. They should create history.

## Examples

- **Discoveries** — a forgotten region becomes known.
- **Conflicts** — factions enter conflict.
- **Environmental Changes** — a region changes due to natural or supernatural
  events.
- **Community Achievements** — players collectively accomplish something that
  changes the world.

## Persistent Consequences

Whenever possible, major events should leave lasting effects: new settlements,
changed landscapes, historical records, new opportunities, altered
relationships. The world should remember.

## Rotating Event Layer

World events are also the home of the rotating/seasonal discovery layer described
in `systems/unlocks-and-discovery.md` — a small set of time-boxed events that
refresh mystery on a cadence, sitting on top of the stable authored world.

**Stretch goal:** a major world event, once conquered by players, could
retroactively unlock things in areas players did not previously know about.
